## Goals

- Tighter mounting
- Faster access to the CVT for maintenance
- A detachable layout that splits into two halves

## Research so far

- Reviewed the team's previous and current case designs
- Learned how a CVT works and what the case has to protect
- Compared materials, and bent versus welded sheet metal construction
- Evaluating latch concepts: a push hook versus an opposing tension latch
