title: Tracing why the vehicle drifted
date: 2025-11-10  # approximate
type: Experiment
project: enes100-otv
summary: One mechanical cause and one electrical cause, found one at a time.
---

The vehicle would not drive straight.

1. **First cause, mechanical.** A drive motor was blocked and dragged one wheel, pulling the vehicle into right angle turns. I redesigned the motor mount and the wheel to shaft connection.
2. **Second cause, motor speed.** With the drag gone, there was still some drift. It came from the two motors running at different speeds.

Fixing the obvious problem first made the smaller one visible.
