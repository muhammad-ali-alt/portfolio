---
title: Change one thing at a time
date: 2026-10-20  # approximate
type: Learned
summary: A business simulation taught me the same rule as a lab experiment.
---

In GLO BUS, a business simulation for BMGT110, I changed too many decisions at once in Year 6. Earnings per share fell from $1.30 to $1.03 and return on equity fell from 20.4% to 16.5%, and I could not tell which change caused it.

After that I changed one thing at a time and checked the result before changing the next. By Year 12 the company scored 90 and tied for 3rd.

It is the same rule as controlling variables in an experiment.
