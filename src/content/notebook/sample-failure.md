**What happened.** The corners of a large flat part lifted off the bed halfway through the print.

**Why.** The bottom layers cooled and shrank faster than the layers above them, which pulled the corners up.

**What I changed.** Added a brim, raised the bed temperature, and turned the part 45 degrees so the long edge was not facing the fan.

**Result.** Second print stayed flat.
