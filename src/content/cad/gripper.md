Single solid body built from an extrusion, cut features, a mirror and two linear patterns (slots and teeth). Modeled following the CADelics 6-Axis Robotic Arm Series tutorial.

Make the number of teeth a variable. My notebook entry showed that fewer teeth bite harder but mark the object more, so the right number depends on what the gripper picks up.
